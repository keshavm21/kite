# Optimizer results: -O0 vs -O1

Instruction counts per example, generated by `scripts/opt_stats.py` (do not edit by hand).
-O1 = `remove-unreachable, ssa, {constfold copyprop gvn dce simplifycfg} x<=4, {unroll constfold copyprop gvn dce simplifycfg} x<=4, licm gvn strength constfold dce simplifycfg, out-of-ssa`.

| example | IR -O0 | IR -O1 | change | PTX -O0 | PTX -O1 | change | regs -O0 | regs -O1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| block_sum | 40 | 37 | -8% | 54 | 51 | -6% | 12 | 13 |
| casts | 30 | 26 | -13% | 42 | 38 | -10% | 13 | 13 |
| collatz_steps | 41 | 44 | +7% | 46 | 50 | +9% | 11 | 12 |
| matmul_naive | 39 | 36 | -8% | 51 | 49 | -4% | 14 | 14 |
| matmul_tiled | 87 | 149 | +71% | 111 | 236 | +113% | 16 | 34 |
| relu | 13 | 12 | -8% | 21 | 20 | -5% | 10 | 10 |
| row_sum | 25 | 23 | -8% | 33 | 32 | -3% | 12 | 12 |
| saxpy | 15 | 14 | -7% | 26 | 25 | -4% | 10 | 10 |
| softmax_rows | 57 | 49 | -14% | 70 | 65 | -7% | 17 | 17 |
| vector_add | 14 | 13 | -7% | 26 | 25 | -4% | 12 | 12 |
| **total** | 361 | 403 | +12% | 480 | 591 | +23% | | |

IR counts are instructions including terminators. PTX counts are body instructions (no `.reg` lines).
A smaller PTX file is not necessarily faster code: ptxas runs its own optimizer, so compare registers and SASS too (`gpu/bench.py`, results in `bench_T4.md`).
