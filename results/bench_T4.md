# Benchmarks on Tesla T4

Generated by `gpu/bench.py` (do not edit by hand). Times are the median of 20 launches after 3 warm-ups, measured with CUDA events. Sizes are realistic: 2^24 elements for saxpy and relu, 1024^3 for matmul.

- GPU: Tesla T4, target `sm_75`, CuPy 14.2.0.
- Percent-of-peak uses the T4 datasheet numbers (320 GB/s, 8100 GFLOP/s fp32); they are a ceiling, not a target.
- GB/s counts the bytes the algorithm must move once (saxpy: read x and y, write y; relu and softmax: read x, write y). `softmax_rows` re-reads rows, so its figure is an *effective* rate.
- GFLOP/s is 2*m*n*k / time.
- Kite arithmetic is `mul.rn`/`add.rn` with no fma. The CUDA C variants use `--fmad=false` so the arithmetic is comparable. The library rows (CuPy, cuBLAS) use whatever they like, including fma and heavily tuned tiling.
- Registers come from the loaded kernel; SASS counts come from `cuobjdump -sass` on the ptxas (Kite) or nvcc (CUDA C) cubin.
- Every variant is compared with the library result first; the last column says whether it matched (`rtol`/`atol` per kernel: exact for relu, 1e-6 for saxpy, 1e-4 for softmax because of `exp`, 1e-3 for matmul because of summation order).
- ptxas re-optimizes the PTX, so a smaller `-O1` PTX does not have to mean faster SASS (docs/PTX_BACKEND.md section 11).

| variant | time (ms) | GB/s | % of T4 peak | regs | SASS insts | matches library |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|:-:|
| Kite -O0 | 0.7699 | 261.5 | 82% | 10 | 16 | yes |
| Kite -O1 without unroll | 0.7706 | 261.2 | 82% | 10 | 16 | yes |
| Kite -O1 | 0.7710 | 261.1 | 82% | 10 | 16 | yes |
| CUDA C (NVRTC) | 0.7700 | 261.5 | 82% | 10 | 16 | yes |
| CuPy `2.5*x+y` (2 kernels, 1 temporary) | 1.3742 | 146.5 | 46% | n/a | n/a | yes |

-O1 vs -O0 runtime: **no measurable change** (0.7699 -> 0.7710 ms), registers 10 -> 10; SASS 16 -> 16 instructions.

Loop unrolling alone (-O1 vs -O1 without unroll): **no measurable change** (0.7706 -> 0.7710 ms), registers 10 -> 10; SASS 16 -> 16 instructions.
